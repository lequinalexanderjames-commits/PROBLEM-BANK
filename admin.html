Troll filter and grouping

Thresholds

Every message gets a risk score from 0 to 100. At or above the flag line it waits for review. At or above the reject line it is hidden automatically.

Lower the similarity number to merge more problems together. Raise it to keep groups tighter. It applies to new responses. Use rebuild below to apply it to old ones.
